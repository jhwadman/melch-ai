# Support

`melchizedek-agents` is an open-source project under the MIT licence,
maintained by one person ([@jhwadman](https://github.com/jhwadman)). There is
no company behind it, no paid support, and no service-level agreement. Plan
your adoption accordingly: you own your deployment, and you can fork the code.

## Where to ask

| You have | Go to |
|---|---|
| A security vulnerability | GitHub private vulnerability reporting, per [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Never a public issue. |
| A bug | A [bug report](https://github.com/jhwadman/melch-ai/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the version, the YAML and the output |
| A feature request or a question | A [feature request](https://github.com/jhwadman/melch-ai/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml), or the docs first: [DOCUMENTATION.md](./DOCUMENTATION.md) and the `wiki/` bundle |

## What to expect

- **Security reports:** acknowledged within 5 working days, with a status
  update at least every 14 days ([SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)).
- **Bugs and requests:** best effort, with no response time promised.
  A report with a failing test or an exact reproduction is the fastest to
  fix.
- **Versions:** what an upgrade may break, and for how long a version gets
  fixes, is in [VERSIONING.md](./VERSIONING.md).
