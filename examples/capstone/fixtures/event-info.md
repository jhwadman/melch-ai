# Elmbrook Makers Fair 2026 — published event information

FICTIONAL: every name, place, time and contact in this file is invented
for the course's capstone. Published by the organisers on 2026-10-01.

## When and where

- Date: Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Doors open at 09:30. The fair closes at 16:00.
- Venue: Elmbrook Community Hall, 12 Mill Lane, Elmbrook.

## Tickets

- Entry is free. Registration on the fair's page is requested so the
  organisers can plan numbers, but walk-ins are welcome.
- Workshops cost 5 credits each, paid at the welcome desk on the day.

## Programme

- 10:00–13:00 Repair café in Room B: bring one small household item
  (lamps, toasters, clothing) and volunteers help you repair it.
- 11:00–12:00 Beginners' soldering workshop in Room A, 12 places.
- 13:30–14:30 Talk in the Main Hall: "Fixing things with your
  neighbours" by the Elmbrook Tool Library.
- 14:00–15:30 Children's craft table in the Main Hall, ages 5–11, with
  an adult present.

## Accessibility

- The main entrance on Mill Lane is step-free, and all rooms are on the
  ground floor.
- The Main Hall has a hearing loop.
- An accessible toilet is next to Room A.

## Food

- The Elmbrook Scouts run a café in the foyer from 10:00 to 15:00, with
  vegetarian and vegan options.

## Getting there

- Bus 7 stops at Mill Lane, two minutes' walk away.
- There are 20 parking spaces behind the hall, 4 of them for blue
  badge holders.

## Rules

- Only assistance dogs are allowed inside.

## Contact

- The organisers: hello@makersfair.example, or the welcome desk on the day.
